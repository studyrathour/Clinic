Our History

Established in 1970

Rajdhani Mattress Mart is proud to be one of Surat's oldest and most deeply trusted mattress and curtain dealers. Located in the bustling area of Palanpur Jakat Naka, our journey began over five decades ago.

Since 1970, we have been unwavering in our mission to provide the local community with exceptional comfort and quality sleep solutions. With over 54 years of dedicated service, we have built a reputation based on trust, quality craftsmanship, and a deep understanding of our customers' needs.

What started as a small, humble store has grown into a cornerstone business in the region. We've watched generations of families return to us, relying on our expertise to furnish their homes with the best mattresses and beautiful curtains.

Our long-standing presence in the market is a testament to our core values: offering genuine products, providing expert advice, and ensuring that every customer leaves satisfied with their purchase.
